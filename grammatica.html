Grammar Rules

Here you will find the main rules of Italian grammar.

Fundamental Rules

1. Articles
Articles determine if the noun is specific or general.
Definite: il, lo, la, i, gli, le
Indefinite: un, uno, una, un'

2. Nouns
They indicate people, things, animals, or ideas.
Masculine: ragazzo, libro
Feminine: ragazza, casa
Plural: ragazzi, case

3. Adjectives
They qualify nouns and agree in gender and number.
Examples: il ragazzo alto, la ragazza alta, i ragazzi alti, le ragazze alte

4. Verbs
They indicate actions or states and are conjugated based on tense, mood, and person.
Parlare (to speak): io parlo, tu parli, lui/lei parla
Essere (to be): io sono, tu sei, lui/lei è
Avere (to have): io ho, tu hai, lui/lei ha

5. Pronouns
They replace nouns.
Personal: io, tu, lui/lei, noi, voi, loro
Possessive: mio, tuo, suo, nostro, vostro, loro
Direct: mi, ti, lo, la, ci, vi, li, le
Indirect: mi, ti, gli, le, ci, vi

6. Prepositions
They connect words and indicate relationships.
Simple: di, a, da, in, con, su, per, tra, fra
Articulated: del, della, al, alla, nel, nella

7. Conjunctions
They connect phrases or words.
Coordinating: e, ma, oppure, quindi
Subordinating: che, perché, se, quando, mentre

8. Interrogatives
Words used to ask questions: chi, cosa, dove, quando, perché

9. Negative Sentences
"Non" is used.
Example: Non mangio la pizza (I don't eat pizza).

10. Accents and Apostrophes
The accent changes meaning: è (verb "to be") vs e (conjunction "and").
Apostrophe: l’amico (instead of lo amico).

11. Word Order
Generally: Subject + Verb + Object.
Example: Io mangio una mela (I eat an apple).
